Grant September 2026

Biointelect Venturer grant awarded to Proboxis Bio

Proboxis Bio was selected for the inaugural Biointelect Venturer cohort and awarded $1.5 million in non-dilutive funding, one of six Australian infectious disease technologies sharing $10.48 million. Supported by the Australian Government through the Medical Research Future Fund, the two-year program provides tailored commercialisation support, expert mentoring, and access to national and international networks. Proboxis Bio is developing a vaccine production platform for mosquito-borne viruses, beginning with Japanese encephalitis, with the potential to support the development of vaccines against other mosquito-borne viruses.
